The Executive Branch

Handles high-level strategic planning, club paperwork, forms, and team management. Oversees either the Community or Projects sub-branch and reports directly in weekly meetings with the President.

President:

Leads overall club vision, leads officer meetings, and acts as the primary external liaison to university's EE head. Responsible for setting semester goals, approving major initiatives, and guiding overall club execution. The most important Officer role. Guides where they want the club to go in, major call's and prescribes goals for other officers.

Vice President:

The president's second in command, helps with officer workflows, major decision making and team communication. Works as an assistant to the President, stepping in during their absence, and ensuring all branch leads are hitting their key given prescripts. This role carries the will of the prescript for Club.

Treasurer:

Manages the club’s financial accounts, budget allocations, and formal funding proposals. Tracks semester income and expenses, coordinates with the President to properly scale the scope of what the Club can and cannot do. an overseer of costs and allocations.

Secretary:

Manages EVERYTHING, making sure no page is left unturned and not one chip uninitated. Takes care of organizing assets created, slideshows given, and documents that need a safe space.

Executive Officer:

For those without a primary Role but carries the will of the prescripts given by the president in meetings, from managing projects, events, and general initatives. The executive officer is the all-rounder who can flex and stretch to do what needs to be completed
